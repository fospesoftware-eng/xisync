02 · CORE FEATURES

Eight core modules covering the full export workflow

Deep intelligence at every stage, from acquisition to delivery

Omnichannel Buyer Inbox

Unify WhatsApp, WeChat, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and email. Real-time bilingual translation; auto-linked buyer profiles and quote history.

AI Trade Assistant

Autonomous negotiation engine prices dynamically by raw material cost, MOQ, and tariffs. Buyer price-sensitivity simulation. Auto contract clauses. Global demand forecasting.

Global Logistics Tracking

Sea, air, and express multimodal tracking with vessel voyage and container status. Spot rate comparison, smart route risk control, customs node mapping.

Global Trade Compliance

Lifecycle management for EU, FCC/FDA, UKCA, Middle East, and IEC certifications with automatic expiry alerts.

Marketplace Sync

One-click import of all Alibaba and Made-in-China listings into your catalog with bidirectional price and inventory sync. SKU mapping, incremental and full sync.

AI Website Builder

Generate multilingual storefronts with Home, Products, Factory, Logistics, Certifications, and Contact templates. Auto catalog sync, SEO metadata, DNS and analytics.

Global Digital Ads

Unified management of Facebook, Google, and TikTok ad accounts. AI creative studio, attribution tracking, ROI dashboards. Agency parent accounts and global kill switch.

Credit Risk Shield

Importer credit scoring, sanctions list screening, wire fraud detection, tariff change alerts, and letter-of-credit review thresholds. Protect every transaction.
